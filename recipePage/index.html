Candied Cheez-It Crackers

Description

Similar to caramel corn, this recipe starts with butter, brown sugar, corn syrup, and baking soda in a saucepan. The caramel mixture is melted down and poured over a tray of Cheez-Its. After coating each cracker in the caramel sauce, you’ll need to bake them until hardened. Then comes the difficult part—letting it cool. During this time, the Candied Cheez-Its firm up, resulting in a cracker that has all the sweetness of caramel corn but with a Cheddar-flavored kick.

In the comments section, it was clear that fans can’t wait to try this unique treat. “The way I ran to get some Cheez-It [crackers]. Impatiently waiting for them to cool as I type,” wrote one user. Many begged the brand for the recipe amounts, while others lamented that they will have to wait until Gluten-Free Cheez-It crackers hit stores.

Ingredients

  • 1 (11.7 ounce) box Cheez-It Crackers
  • 1 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1 stick unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup light corn syrup
  • Salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 250 degrees F (120 degrees C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Spread crackers across the prepared baking sheet. Set aside.
  3. Combine brown sugar, butter, corn syrup, and a pinch of salt in a saucepan over medium heat; stir until sugar has dissolved and mixture is smooth. Off heat, whisk in baking soda.
  4. Drizzle butter mixture evenly over crackers while working quickly, using a rubber spatula to ensure each cracker is coated; arrange in a single layer.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 1 hour, gently stirring every 15 minutes. Cool crackers completely before serving. Store leftovers at room temperature in an airtight container.
